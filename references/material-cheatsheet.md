# Material Cheatsheet

Use this as a fast reference after the main skill has identified the real material.

| Material | Base color behavior | Roughness | Normal / height | Common realism cues | Common failure |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Painted plaster | muted, lightly varied | mostly matte | fine grain, shallow unevenness | repairs, rain streaks, splash dirt | flat color + generic noise |
| Stucco | mineral/painted tone | matte to semi-matte | aggregate/trowel relief | irregular finish, localized wear | over-cratered bump |
| Granite | mineral family, not one flat color | varies by finish | crystalline/granular | cut-face detail, small chips | painted speckles |
| Sandstone/limestone | earthy/mineral | usually matte | porous/layered | erosion at exposed edges | plastic slab look |
| Brick | restrained brick family | matte | mortar recession | brick-to-brick variation | tiled photo wallpaper |
| Concrete | gray/tinted mineral | matte | pores, casting variation | subtle formwork, stains | smooth gray plane |
| Painted wood | coating color + subtle substrate influence | coating dependent | grain/fiber beneath paint | edge/contact wear | plastic gloss |
| Bare wood | species-dependent | matte/semi | grain, pores, fibers | directional grain | giant/repeating grain |
| Painted metal | coating color | coating dependent | mostly coating microdetail | scratches/chips where plausible | treating paint as metallic |
| Bare metal | conductor-specific | finish dependent | machining/oxidation detail | anisotropy or oxidation if justified | universal chrome |
| Glass | near-colorless/tinted | reflection dependent | normally minimal | angle reflection, interior depth | black rectangles/mirrors |
| Asphalt | dark gray, varied | matte dry / lower wet | aggregate | patches, tire wear, wet zones | smooth gray floor |
| Concrete paving | mineral tone | matte | pores/joints | dirt at joints/edges | giant noisy bump |

## Weathering placement

Prefer weathering at:

- sill runoff
- cornice drip lines
- wall bases
- drain outlets
- joints and repairs
- touched edges
- outward corners
- traffic/contact zones

Do not distribute damage uniformly.

## Depth hierarchy

Use this test:

- changes silhouette / casts visible profile → geometry
- visible parallax / deep material recess → displacement/height
- medium surface relief → normal
- tiny optical breakup → normal/roughness
